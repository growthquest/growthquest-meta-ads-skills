# Iteration Guide (Mode 2)

When the user provides performance data, follow this process.

## Step 1: Analyze Winners

Look at top-performing creative (by CTR, conversion rate, or ROAS) and identify:
- **Winning themes** - What topics or pain points appear in top performers?
- **Winning structures** - Questions? Statements? Commands? Numbers?
- **Winning word patterns** - Specific words or phrases that recur?
- **Character utilization** - Are top performers shorter or longer?

## Step 2: Analyze Losers

Look at worst performers and identify:
- **Themes that fall flat** - What angles aren't resonating?
- **Common patterns** - Too generic? Too long? Wrong tone?

## Step 3: Generate New Variations

Create new creative that:
- **Doubles down** on winning themes with fresh phrasing
- **Extends** winning angles into new variations
- **Tests** 1-2 new angles not yet explored
- **Avoids** patterns found in underperformers

## Step 4: Document the Iteration

```
## Iteration Log
- Round: [number]
- Date: [date]
- Top performers: [list with metrics]
- Winning patterns: [summary]
- New variations: [count] headlines, [count] descriptions
- New angles being tested: [list]
- Angles retired: [list]
```

## Batch Generation Workflow

For large-scale production (100+ variations per cycle):

1. **Break into sub-tasks**: Headlines (CTR), Descriptions (conversion), Primary text (engagement)
2. **Generate in waves**: Wave 1 core angles (3-5 x 5 each), Wave 2 extend top 2, Wave 3 wild cards
3. **Quality filter**: Remove over-limit, duplicates, policy violations, bad combos

## Writing Quality Standards

**Headlines:** Specific > vague, benefits > features, active > passive, include numbers.
**Avoid:** Jargon, unsubstantiated claims, all caps, clickbait.
**Descriptions:** Complement headlines (don't repeat), add proof points, handle objections, reinforce CTAs.
